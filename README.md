## 🕹️ About

VOID DIVER: Escape from the Abyss is a 2.5D pixel-art extraction action RPG set in an anomalous city swallowed by the abyss. Manage the BALUSHA antique shop, send Divers into distorted dungeon spaces, recover artifacts, fight aberrations, and make it back alive. Developed by STUDIO NEMO and published by LoadComplete, this repository contains a raw Windows pre-release build that surfaced ahead of schedule.

Built for players who enjoy pixel-art action RPGs, high-risk extraction runs, dungeon crawling, survival horror, and chaotic co-op.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Anomalous City** — Explore familiar streets twisted into dangerous otherworldly dungeon spaces.
- ⚔️ **Real-Time Combat** — Fight aberrations, machines, urban legends, and humans driven mad.
- 💰 **Artifact Recovery** — Recover valuable relics and forbidden devices from the depths.
- 🏪 **BALUSHA Shop** — Manage an antique shop between dangerous expeditions.
- 🧭 **Extraction Runs** — Complete requests, secure your haul, and escape before the abyss takes it.
- 👥 **Solo or Co-op** — Dive alone or cooperate online with up to three other players.
- 🎭 **Distinct Divers** — Choose characters with different abilities, combat styles, and stories.
- 🧪 **Loot and Progression** — Collect weapons, accessories, materials, valuables, and blueprints.
- 🕳️ **Abyssal Threats** — Survive a hostile world filled with madness, curses, and unpredictable enemies.
